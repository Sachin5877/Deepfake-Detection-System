# Day 2

## Goal
Research deepfake datasets.

## Datasets to Explore
1. FaceForensics++


### Dataset Information
- Total Files: 7010
- Total Videos: 7000
- Real Videos: 1000
- Fake Videos: 6000

### Manipulation Methods
1. DeepFakeDetection - 1000 fake videos
2. Deepfakes - 1000 fake videos
3. Face2Face - 1000 fake videos
4. FaceShifter - 1000 fake videos
5. FaceSwap - 1000 fake videos
6. NeuralTextures - 1000 fake videos

### Other Files
- CSV Metadata Files: 10

### Download Method
### Download Method
- Downloaded from Kaggle.
- Available as a ZIP file.
- Extract the ZIP file after downloading to access the dataset folders and videos.

### Storage Required
- Approximately 18 GB

### Advantages
- Contains multiple deepfake generation techniques.
- Large number of fake videos.
- Popular benchmark dataset for deepfake detection research.

### Disadvantages
- Requires considerable storage space.
- Some videos are compressed, which may affect quality.

2. Celeb-DF

### Dataset Information
- Total Videos: 6229
- Real Videos: 590
- Fake Videos: 5639

### Description
- Contains high-quality DeepFake videos with visual quality similar to videos found online.
- Includes subjects of different ages, genders, and ethnic groups.
- Extended version of Celeb-DF (v1), which contained 795 DeepFake videos.

### Download Method
- Downloaded from Kaggle.
- Available as a ZIP file.
- Extract after downloading.

### Storage Required
- Approximately 10 GB

### Advantages
- High-quality and realistic DeepFake videos.
- Large number of fake samples.
- More challenging and realistic than many earlier datasets.

### Disadvantages
- Fewer real videos compared to fake videos.
- Focuses mainly on face-swapping DeepFakes.

3. DFDC (Deepfake Detection Challenge)

### Dataset Information
- Total Size: Approximately 100 GB
- Video Formats: MP4, AVI, MOV
- Video Duration: 2–10 seconds
- Resolution: 480p–1080p
- Labels: Real / Fake

### Content Distribution
- Real Videos: Approximately 50%
- Fake Videos: Approximately 50%
- Manipulation Types:
  - Face Swapping
  - Facial Reenactment
  - Speech-Driven Animation

### Download Method
- Downloaded from Kaggle.
- Available as 10 ZIP files (00.zip–09.zip).
- Each ZIP file is approximately 10 GB.

### Storage Required
- Approximately 100 GB

### Advantages
- Very large dataset.
- Balanced real and fake samples.
- Contains multiple deepfake techniques.
- Suitable for training robust deepfake detection models.

### Disadvantages
- Requires significant storage space.
- Requires a powerful GPU and sufficient RAM for training.
- Processing videos can take considerable time.
